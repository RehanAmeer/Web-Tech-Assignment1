Our story

Started at a kitchen table, still run the same way

Moonpure began as a small side project — sourcing a few kilos of almonds and walnuts directly from local suppliers for friends and family who were tired of stale, overpriced dry fruit from general stores.

Word spread, and the small side project turned into a proper store. What hasn't changed is how we work: small batches, hand-checked quality, and honest weight in every pack, whether it's a 250g pouch or a full wedding hamper.

What guides us

Our values

Freshness first

We restock in small quantities rather than holding large inventory, so nothing sits around long enough to lose flavour.

Fair pricing

Prices are set close to what we pay our suppliers — no inflated "gift shop" markup on the hampers either.

Real communication

Every order is confirmed by a real person on WhatsApp, not an automated system. You can always ask before you buy.

From orchard to your door

How an order comes together

Sourcing

We buy directly from local growers and trusted wholesalers in small weekly batches.

Sorting & cleaning

Every batch is hand-sorted to remove broken pieces, shells and debris.

Weighing & packing

Orders are weighed precisely and sealed in food-safe packaging the same week.

Nationwide delivery

Handed to our courier partners and tracked until it reaches your door.
